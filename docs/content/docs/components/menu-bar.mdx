A row of titles (`File`, `Edit`, `View`) or, with `compact(true)`, one hamburger button
whose panel lists the same titles as submenus. Panels cascade to the right, nest to any
depth and flip to the left when the window has no room.

```rust
use zaxis::{MenuBar, MenuItem};

let items = [
    MenuItem::submenu("File", [
        MenuItem::new("new", "New").shortcut("Ctrl+N"),
        MenuItem::submenu("Open recent", [MenuItem::new("r1", "notes.txt")]),
        MenuItem::separator(),
        MenuItem::new("autosave", "Autosave").checked(self.autosave),
    ]),
    MenuItem::submenu("Help", [MenuItem::new("about", "About")]),
];
if let Some(id) = MenuBar::new("main-menu", &items).compact(true).show(ui).selected {
    // Exactly one activation per gesture; the menu has already closed.
}
```

`ui.menu_bar(source, &items)` is the one-expression form of the horizontal bar.

- **Pointer:** a press opens a title; moving across the other titles switches the menu;
  hovering a row with a chevron opens its submenu; a press on the open title, outside the
  panels or Escape closes it.
- **Keyboard:** Up/Down/Home/End move over enabled rows, Right or Enter enter a submenu,
  Left leaves it or, on the first level, switches to the neighbouring title, Enter on an
  action activates it.
- **Items:** `MenuItem::new(id, text)` with a stable `Id`, `submenu(text, items)`,
  `separator()`, `.shortcut(text)`, `.checked(bool)` and `.enabled(bool)`. Disabled rows
  and titles are skipped by the keyboard and ignore presses. A top-level action in the bar
  activates directly.
- **Style:** `.style(ContextMenuStyle)` — menu bars share the proportions and appearance of
  context menus.

Shortcut captions and check marks are display state: the application owns the shortcut
binding and toggles its own flags in response to `selected`.

## Limitations

- One menu bar is open at a time (it uses the context's single popup).
- Submenus open immediately on hover, without a delay or a pointer-direction tolerance.
- No icons, radio groups or custom widgets inside rows; no Alt-key mnemonics or `F10`
  focus; no OS-native menu bar.
